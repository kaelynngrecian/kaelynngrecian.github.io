“Pain is a complex physiological and behavioral response that engages the nervous system at many levels, from sensory nerve endings in the skin to emotional centers deep within the brain.”

— David Julius, Nobel Prize 2021

Peripheral sensitization is a form of functional plasticity in primary afferent nociceptors, in which inflammatory mediators lower activation thresholds and heighten responsiveness to stimuli. As a result of repeated inflammatory stimulation, there are durable shifts in nociceptor activation thresholds. Accordingly, I am interested in investigating which mechanisms, if any, allow long-term plasticity to drive persistent nociceptor sensitization underlying chronic pain.
